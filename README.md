## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ Bulletproof Auto Farm
- **Auto Farm Level**: Smart quest auto-progression that naturally completes 5/5 mob quests, transitions seamlessly across all 34 quest tiers (Lv. 1 to Lv. 35,000+), and never stalls or loops.
- **Auto Farm Specific Mob**: Select any mob from the complete directory of 37 spawners.
- **Kill NPC in Sky (Sky Mode)**: Calibrated hover at 5.8 studs directly above mobs with zero-velocity lock. 100% melee reach with zero damage taken from enemies.
- **Mob Magnet**: Pulls all spawned mobs of the target type into a single stack for instant multi-kill.
- **Fast Attack**: Multi-hit burst packet delivery with automatic tool swing animation cancellation to eliminate debounce locks.
- **Auto Skills**: Rotates Z, X, C, and V moves every 0.8s for maximum AoE clearance.
- **Auto Buso Haki**: Automatically keeps Armament Haki activated.

### 🛠️ Weapon & Item Crafting Automation
- **Auto Farm Materials for Weapon**: Select any weapon (Tanto, Katana, Boxing, Karate Fish, Black Leg, SSJ, Cid, Yamato, etc.) — the hub inspects your inventory, identifies missing ingredients, automatically farms the corresponding mob, and crafts the weapon as soon as materials are collected!
- **Auto Craft Affordable Weapons & Items**: Continuously monitors inventory and crafts any weapon or table item as soon as you have the ingredients.
- **Auto Equip Best Weapon**: Periodically equips your highest rarity owned weapon (Mythical / Legendary / Epic / Rare).
- **One-Click Craft All**: Single button to craft everything currently affordable in your inventory.

### 📈 Stats & Player Enhancements
- **Auto Distribute Stats**: Automatically allocates stat points into Melee, Defense, Sword, or Power.
- **Speed & Jump Bypasses**: Customizable WalkSpeed, JumpPower, and Infinite Jump.
- **Fly Mode**: Smooth WASD + Space/Shift flying.
- **Anti-Stun & Anti-Void Shield**: Clears stun/freeze attributes and prevents ocean or void death.

### 🗺️ Teleports & Visuals
- **All Islands Teleport**: Instant one-click teleportation to all 23 islands and special zones (Tournament of Power, Space, Raids, Dungeons).
- **NPCs & Bosses Teleport**: Direct teleports to Mihawk, Ryuma, Lucci, Katana Man, Cid Man, etc.
- **Visuals / ESP**: Player ESP, Mob ESP with live health bars, Chest ESP.
- **Redeem Codes**: One-click redemption of all active game codes.
